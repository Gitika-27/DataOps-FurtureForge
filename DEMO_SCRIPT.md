## Operator Safety Tips for Live Pitch
1. **Wi-Fi Down?** The Synthetic Demo runs completely offline from local backend memory or snapshot.
2. **Short on time?** Press `Esc` during the 7-second animation to jump straight to the verified cut state.
3. **Judges asking for ledger?** Click **"Ledger Report"** in the top bar to show the escaped HTML ledger and PDF.
